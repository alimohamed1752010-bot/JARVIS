# JARVIS V8.3.6 — Voice Move Clarification Fix

- Clarification replies (`1`, `gen 1`, `general 1`, `jarvis general 1`) now execute against the already-selected Discord channel ID.
- Fixed the executor incorrectly reparsing a bare clarification reply and returning `Which voice channel should I move them to`.
- Voice-channel matching now uses normalized exact names before partial matching, so `General 1` does not collide with `Secret General 1`.
- Pending clarification remains owner/guild scoped and expires after 60 seconds.
- Unrelated messages are not consumed by the pending clarification handler.
