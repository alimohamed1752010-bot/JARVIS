# JARVIS V8.2.2

- Robust member-name resolution for owner actions (exact, prefix, word, and unique partial matches).
- `jarvis timeout Steve` defaults to 10 minutes if no duration is supplied.
- `jarvis insult Steve` no longer requires a mention.
- `jarvis Steve is spamming, handle it` infers a 10-minute timeout.
- `jarvis untime Steve out`, `jarvis untime Steve`, and `jarvis untimeout Steve` remove an active timeout.
- Timeout removal is detected before fresh timeout actions.
- Owner natural-language moderation remains protected by permission, hierarchy, owner, server-owner, and self checks.
