# JARVIS V18.5

## Spotify natural-language routing fix

Fixed a V18.3 routing regression where natural requests such as:

- `jarvis play into it on spotify`
- `jarvis play "Into It" on Spotify`
- `jarvis play into it in spotify`

could be interpreted by generic application discovery as an attempt to launch an app named `into it on spotify`.

V18.5 keeps Spotify playback as a dedicated `pc_spotify_play` action and strips the trailing `on Spotify` / `in Spotify` platform phrase from the track query.

`put on Spotify` and `open Spotify` continue to launch Spotify normally.

## Base

Built directly from the verified JARVIS V18.3 package. Existing PC, Discord, awareness, browser, app/game, volume, file, and Spotify systems are preserved.
